Good to know

Questions

What exactly am I paying today?

A refundable deposit that reserves your size from the limited run. The remaining balance is only charged when your pair is confirmed and ready to ship.

Is the deposit really refundable?

Yes. You can request a full refund of your deposit any time before your pair enters production. Just reply to your Stripe receipt or email us.

How does sizing work?

These are unisex and run true to standard Nike Air Max 90 sizing. Use the Men's / Women's toggle to shop your scale — women's US is 1.5 sizes up from men's (e.g. Men's 8 = Women's 9.5). If you're between sizes, we suggest sizing up. Your size is confirmed at checkout as part of your order.

When will they ship?

This is a made-to-order limited run. Current estimate is Q4 2026. You'll get email updates at each milestone and can cancel for a full deposit refund before production.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — shipping and any duties are calculated on the balance invoice based on your address.
